Advanced Survey Structure 25 mins

  • Sequencing & branching logic
  • Minimizing respondent fatigue
+

Key Terms

Sequencing: Ordering questions logically to guide respondents.

Branching Logic: Displaying different questions based on previous answers.

Respondent Fatigue: When participants lose interest due to overly long or repetitive surveys.

Discussion Points

  • Why does question sequence matter for data quality?
  • How might branching logic improve participant engagement?
  • What are early signs of respondent fatigue, and how can we prevent it?

Activity

Sketch a small branching flow diagram for a hypothetical survey topic. Show how participants might skip or dive deeper into sections based on their answers.

Reflection Questions

  • What potential issues arise if branching logic isn’t set up correctly?
  • How can you test your survey flow before launching it?

AI Tools for Survey Design 25 mins

  • Demonstration of AI-assisted question generation
  • Evaluating clarity & bias
+

Key Terms

AI Question Generation: Automated suggestions for question wording or structure.

Template Adaptation: Using existing question sets to speed up survey creation.

Discussion Points

  • Which parts of the survey process benefit most from AI suggestions?
  • What limitations or potential biases might AI introduce?

Activity

Use an AI extension or plugin with Google Forms (or a similar platform) to generate at least two new survey questions. Edit them to ensure neutrality, clarity, and alignment with your research goals.

Reflection Questions

  • How did AI-generated questions differ from what you’d write on your own?
  • Why is human oversight still crucial when using AI for question design?

QR Code Creation & Distribution 20 mins

  • Generating QR codes for survey access
  • Digital vs. printed distribution strategies
+

Key Terms

QR Code: Quick Response code that links to online forms or websites.

Engagement Rate: How many people scan/visit your survey compared to how many see the code.

Discussion Points

  • Why might QR codes be appealing to certain audiences?
  • In which locations or platforms are QR codes most effective?

Activity

Generate a QR code for a mock or real survey link. Brainstorm potential places to display or share it (e.g., flyers, social media, local businesses), and how you’ll track responses.

Reflection Questions

  • Which distribution methods might resonate most with your community?
  • What other ways could you quickly share your survey link?

Assignment

Draft Three AI-Enhanced Survey Questions In-Class Activity

  • Use an AI tool for question generation
  • Provide a brief rationale for each question
  • Check for clarity, neutrality, and ethics
 

Instructions: Propose three original survey questions relevant to your community needs assessment. Each question should be run through an AI assistant (or tool) for generation or refinement. For each, include a sentence explaining why it’s important and how it aligns with best practices on ethics and bias. Submit your draft by the next session for feedback.
